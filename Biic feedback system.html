Process

Simple, transparent process

Three steps between your experience and meaningful change inside BIIC.

01

Sign In Securely

Log in with your BIIC employee account. Registration takes under a minute.

02

Submit Your Feedback

Choose complaint or compliment, select a category, and write your message.

03

Admin Reviews & Acts

Management reviews every submission, updates its status, and takes appropriate action.

Features

Built for accountability

Everything needed for a fair, functional internal feedback system.

Named & Accountable

Submissions are linked to your BIIC account so management can follow up appropriately.

Admin-Only Visibility

Submissions are never visible to other employees. Only authenticated administrators can access the dashboard.

Full Status Tracking

Every submission moves through New → In Review → Resolved. Track your own submissions in real time.

Categorised Submissions

Filter by department, type, or status. Spot patterns and take systemic action before issues escalate.
